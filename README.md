## 📊 Research Projects

### [Relationship Dissolution & Social Cognition](https://github.com/MillieMeng/relationship-dissolution-analysis)
Mixed-methods study on why people blame themselves when someone disappears without explanation ("ghosting"). Survey **N=119** + qualitative interviews **n=19**. EFA confirmed high-reliability scale (α=.891, KMO=.812); **62.9%** showed elevated rumination with no significant gender differences. Developed a "Narrative Vacuum" framework to explain the cognitive shift from situational curiosity to dispositional self-doubt.

`R` `EFA` `psychometrics` `mixed-methods` `ggplot2`

### [Behavioral Data Analysis — Cognitive Control (Flanker Task)](https://github.com/MillieMeng/cognitive-control-flanker)
End-to-end R pipeline processing **8,000+ behavioral observations**. Cleans and reshapes trial-level data, builds visualizations across experimental conditions, and applies LOESS regression to surface response-time trends. Reproducible analytics workflow from raw data to documented findings.

`R` `tidyverse` `LOESS regression` `data wrangling`

### [Predictive Modeling — Attachment & Depression](https://github.com/MillieMeng/attachment-self-compassion-study)
Regression-based analysis on archival survey data **(n=473)**. Multiple linear regression explains **30.6% of variance** in depression scores. Includes full assumption testing, two-way ANOVA, independent samples t-test, and APA write-up.

`R` `multiple regression` `ANOVA` `RMarkdown`
